## Why does naming matter?

Good naming makes code readable, maintainable, and predictable. It reduces cognitive load, helps new team members onboard faster, and prevents bugs caused by misunderstanding what a variable, function, or class is supposed to do. A well-chosen name is the simplest form of documentation.
